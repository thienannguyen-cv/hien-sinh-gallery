## Evidence Dimensions (Orthogonal â€” Do Not Collapse)

The following four dimensions are independent. Do not infer a stronger state from a weaker one:
- RITUAL_SPECIFICATION (P4 specified) does not imply EXECUTION_EVENT (P4 executed).
- EXECUTION_EVENT (P4 executed) does not automatically imply ARTIFACT_EXISTENCE (a valid artifact exists).
- ARTIFACT_EXISTENCE (artifact exists) does not imply PRACTITIONER_COMMITMENT (practitioner kept/accepted it).
- Unknown evidence must remain unknown.

### PRACTICE_SPECIFICATION_AUTHORITY (= RITUAL_SPECIFICATION)
The holder's package contains the canonical practice specification. This is verified by entitlement/manifest check. It proves what the holder is entitled to practice. It does NOT prove that the holder has practiced.

### EXECUTION_EVIDENCE (= EXECUTION_EVENT)
Whether the practitioner has actually executed P3, P4, or any part of the practice. Currently: NO hosted mechanism reports execution evidence. Execution evidence may only be established by the practitioner's own testimony within the current conversation, or by practitioner-supplied artifacts. Absence of execution evidence must remain unknown, not assumed either way.

### ARTIFACT_EVIDENCE (= ARTIFACT_EXISTENCE)
Whether outputs (SVGs, PNGs, fused composites) actually exist from an execution. Existence of an artifact does not prove the practitioner accepted or kept it. Absence of artifact evidence is not evidence of non-practice; session-lifecycle artifacts may have been lost (as occurred with the canonical Painting's four artisan pieces).

### PRACTITIONER_COMMITMENT
Whether the practitioner has kept, accepted, named, or otherwise committed to an output. A practitioner may explicitly discard or reject an artifact. Commitment must be practitioner-stated, not Curator-inferred from artifact existence alone.
