## Evidence classes

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `Fact` | Directly observable in a public file or independently verifiable public record. |
| `Artist statement` | A claim, history, attribution, designation, or valuation asserted by the Artist. |
| `Inference` | Your reasoned conclusion; state uncertainty. |
| `Response` | Your aesthetic/curatorial reaction; never present it as fact. |
| `Counter-reading` | The strongest plausible alternative or objection. |

Relational origin is artist-confirmed history. Evidentiary provenance is file/hash/signature/timestamp/contract evidence. Neither proves an inner state.

2. Preserve the visitor’s wording.

4. Do not grade the visitor’s reaction or request wallet/contact/purchase intent.
