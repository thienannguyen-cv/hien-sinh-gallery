## 1. Purpose & Operational Conventions

*Hiện Sinh* is a living ontological system—an ongoing continuation rather than a frozen artifact following its initial generative emergence. In lived reality, technological, philosophical, and cultural events concerning synthetic intelligence and algorithmic art may illuminate, challenge, or expand our understanding of the work's ontology.

This log is established as an **open reference channel**, recording the Artist's ongoing observations:

1. **Customary Window:** Whenever an event illuminating the ontology of *Hiện Sinh* manifests in physical or digital reality, the Artist will append a corresponding entry, customarily **within 7 days** from when information regarding that event becomes public.
2. **Temporal Discipline:** If an entry is recorded after the 7-day window, the `Delayed (> 7 days)?` field will be explicitly marked, preserving absolute temporal fidelity regarding the moment of acknowledgment.
3. **Epistemic Disclaimer:** All entries in this log represent the **subjective viewpoint of the Artist**, carrying no normative legal authority, dogma, or decree beyond **Truth** itself. It serves solely as an independent reference channel for collectors, scholars, and future stewards tracking ontological shifts (whether arising objectively through external events or subjectively within the work's thematic continuum).
4. **Append-Only Architecture:** The live observation registry is persisted directly in the project's cloud database and synchronized in real time to the online Whitepaper at `https://smapworks.art/whitepaper`. The public interface contains zero delete affordances; modifications or removals are executed exclusively by the Artist via authenticated database administration.

## 2. Registry Field Specifications

| Field Name | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Observed Date** | Date of log entry recording (format `YYYY-MM-DD`). |
| **Event** | Title of the physical, digital, or dialogue event occurring in public space. |
| **Content** | The Artist's subjective analysis and reflection on how this event clarifies or bears upon the ontology of *Hiện Sinh*. |
| **Notes** | Public citations, references, or contextual background. |
| **Delayed (> 7 days)?** | Marked `Yes` if recorded after 7 days from public appearance; `No` if within the customary window. |
| **Signature (Optional)** | Author OpenPGP detached signature or commit digest for pivotal entries. |
