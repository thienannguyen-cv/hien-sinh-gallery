# Schedule — Frame Practice

**English access rendering. The Vietnamese `SCHEDULE-FRAME.md` is canonical and governs if the versions differ.** This Schedule supplements `LEGAL-TERMS.en.md`.

## 1. Relation to the Frame

A Frame collector is the practitioner-bearer of one of 8 independently issued Frames (#01–04, #06–09) or Frame 05 in Package 05 Complete. The Frame is a complete symbolic practice configuration, not an incomplete version of Package 05, not a logo pack, and not a physical decorative frame. A practice creates a new event and Output; it does not reproduce the canonical event embodied by the Painting.

This Schedule serves as the pre-purchase public legal projection. A buyer is entitled to access and understand the full scope of use rights, Output rights, and legal restrictions prior to transaction, in accordance with the normative information-distribution boundary established in `WORK-ONTOLOGY.md`. The following clauses state legally effective permissions.

## 2. License while holding the token

To the extent the Artist can grant the rights, the Holder receives a worldwide, non-exclusive license, inseparable from the token and free of additional royalty, to:

- read, store, back up, and practise the Frame;
- modify slots and inputs for their practice;
- create, display, exhibit, publish, and communicate Outputs;
- commercially exploit Outputs the Holder creates;
- document, research, teach, and critique the Frame;
- use reasonable excerpts to describe Output provenance;
- transfer the Frame Token with its valid package and lineage.

The Frame-package license ends when the Holder ceases to hold the token, except for surviving rights in valid Outputs under section 3 and legal evidentiary retention.

## 3. Output rights survive

After selling the Frame Token, a former Holder retains the license already granted for Outputs validly created while they held it. The Artist claims no rights in an Output merely because the Frame was used.

To the extent permitted by law:

- the creator retains rights in their original contributions;
- the Artist's commercial license for the Output is non-exclusive and perpetual;
- those rights do not make the Frame, canonical seeds, prompt package, or "Hiện sinh" the Output creator's work;
- there is no warranty that AI output is itself copyrightable or free of third-party claims.

## 4. Rights not granted

The Holder may not:

- sell, sublicense, publish, or repackage the Frame template, prompt sequence, seed package, validation package, or Buyer Curator handoff itself as a competing edition/framework;
- remint the Frame, Painting, or package copy as a new original NFT;
- claim authorship of "Hiện sinh" or strip/falsify provenance;
- present an Output as the canonical Painting, an official continuation, or an Artist-approved derivative without separate confirmation;
- use Output rights to block the Artist or other Frame holders from practising the Frame;
- disclose material outside the valid package they received.

These restrictions do not prohibit description, review, fair quotation, or presentation of an Output's process.

## 5. Transfer

When the Frame Token transfers:

- the Frame-package license moves to the new Holder;
- the transferor delivers the corresponding package and manifest;
- the transferor's valid pre-existing Output rights survive;
- the transferor may no longer distribute the Frame package or represent themselves as current practitioner-bearer.

The legal framework (`LEGAL-TERMS.md` and this Schedule) transfers intact with the token without modification, re-signing, or Artist approval. The new Holder inherits exactly the rights and limitations specified herein, no more and no less.

## 6. Attribution

Where reasonable, an Output should state:

> Created by [Practitioner Name] through a licensed "Hiện sinh" Frame practice; not the canonical Painting.

Attribution does not create co-authorship with the Artist or imply Artist endorsement.
