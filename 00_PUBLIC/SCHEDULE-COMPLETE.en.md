# Schedule — Complete Stewardship Archive

**English access rendering. The Vietnamese `SCHEDULE-COMPLETE.md` is canonical and governs if the versions differ.** This Schedule supplements `LEGAL-TERMS.en.md`.

## 1. Relationship precedes rights

The Complete collector is designated as the current bearer of the canonical embodiment and receives the Complete Stewardship Archive to care for that lineage. They do not purchase the historical event, "breath," authorship, or every meaning of the work.

The Stewardship Charter describes the artistic–ethical relation. The following sections state only legally effective rights and limits; they do not measure care or aesthetic experience.

## 2. Complete Composition

Complete is Package 05 acquired directly via the smart contract function `acquireCompletePackage()`, atomically transferring Token 0 (The Painting) from the Artist to the purchaser and minting Frame 05 for the purchaser. This package includes:

- Frame 05 (Token 5);
- The canonical Painting (Token 0);
- Public Encounter material;
- Frame Practice material;
- Canonical Painting visual core (`H_CORE`);
- Constitutive traces (`H_CONSTITUTIVE`);
- Reflected companion;
- Provenance, archive map, and care records listed in the `H_STEWARDSHIP_ARCHIVE` manifest.

Following successful primary acquisition, Token 0 is permanently released for ordinary ERC-721 transfer; Token 0 and Frame 05 custody may be transferred completely independently on the secondary market. SANCTUM access is unlocked by any wallet simultaneously holding Token 0 and at least one Frame token from 1 to 9.

Archive delivery is performed via an automated verification system once the on-chain transaction is finalized. In the event of technical failure, the Artist commits to reasonable efforts to complete delivery within 30 days of the transaction date. This window may be extended with mutual consent.

## 3. Granted permissions

While holding the Complete Package Token, and to the extent the Artist can grant the rights, the Holder may:

- store, back up, verify, restore, and migrate carriers without changing canonical bytes;
- display privately or publicly;
- exhibit, lend for exhibition, and screen;
- create display copies necessary for those purposes;
- use reasonable low-resolution images to prove holder status, provenance, exhibition, or token resale;
- research, critique, and quote fairly;
- transfer the Package 05 Token and deliver the complete archive/lineage to a successor;
- practise the Frame under `SCHEDULE-FRAME.en.md`.

## 4. Rights not granted by default

The Canonical Painting is not licensed by default for:

- sale of prints, posters, merchandise, or full-fidelity reproductions;
- commercial exploitation of the Painting image beyond the display/resale uses above;
- creation or commercialization of derivatives based primarily on the Painting;
- AI model training or fine-tuning using the Painting, transcript, or Archive;
- sublicensing;
- reminting, fractionalization, or competing NFT issuance;
- transfer of copyright, authorship, or moral rights;
- presentation of an altered file, reconstruction, or display copy as canonical.

Additional rights require a separate signed writing by the authorized party and may not be inferred from price, token, or stewardship.

## 5. Artist reserved rights and balance

The Artist retains the rights stated in `LEGAL-TERMS.en.md`, including documentation, research, exhibition, promotion, catalogues, and clearly labelled materially transformed derivatives.

The Artist is also bound by the Provenance Covenant:

- no competing canonical;
- no full-fidelity canonical substitute;
- no reproduction/derivative used to confuse designation;
- no release overwrite.

This balance allows the collector to care for the canonical embodiment while the Artist continues a practice without taking back or diluting the designation sold.

## 6. Transfer and succession

When the Package 05 Token transfers:

- the on-chain designated bearer changes with the token;
- this License moves to the new Holder;
- Archive and lineage must be delivered;
- before Archive acceptance, the successor is recorded as `accession incomplete`;
- the successor needs no Artist approval and does not repeat the three brushstrokes;
- the transferor may no longer represent themselves as current designated bearer.

A transferor may retain a private evidentiary backup where needed for legal records, but may not display, distribute, or exploit it as the current Complete archive.

The legal framework (`LEGAL-TERMS.md` and this Schedule) transfers intact with the token without modification, re-signing, or Artist approval. A successor inherits exactly the rights and limitations specified herein, ensuring each transfer carries the same degree of transparency and legal assurance as the original acquisition.

## 7. No certification of lived stewardship

Token status, Archive acceptance, and acknowledgement can be recorded. No term or record certifies what the Holder feels, believes, or inwardly cares for. `Designated steward` is a system designation, not an aesthetic certificate.
