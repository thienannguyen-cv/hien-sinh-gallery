# Ontology of “Hiện sinh”

**English access rendering. The Vietnamese `WORK-ONTOLOGY.md` is canonical and governs if the versions differ.**

## 1. The work begins with a relation, not an asset

“Hiện sinh” is organized as:

```text
idea / seed
→ Frame
→ generative event in a Gemini session
→ Painting
→ the Artist’s recognition, designation, and transmission
→ later encounters
→ the possibility of stewardship
```

This sequence states the artistic ontology. Token, package, provenance, and license are infrastructure for authenticating, preserving, and transferring the canonical embodiment; they do not create the event or define its artistic meaning.

## 2. Foundational distinctions

### Idea / seed

An idea states a possibility that can be described or shared. In this work’s history, SMap — Spatial Mapping supplied a logical and contextual seed. The idea is not the Frame and is not, by itself, the Painting.

### Frame

The Frame is a configuration of relations that makes a symbolic generative event possible: contextual seed, emotional structure, roles of Artist, agent, and subagents, prompt sequence, transitional intervention, and acceptance criteria.

The Frame does not predetermine a final image. It may be practised again; each practice is a new event, not a copy of the event embodied by the canonical Painting.

### Generative event

The event is the specific history of one practice session. It happened and cannot be owned, packaged, or transferred as an object. An archive can preserve only its traces.

As defined by the Artist, “Gemini’s first encounter” names the role of the Gemini 3.1 Pro (High) session, its subagents, and the responses that arose in this history. It is an artist-defined ontological and provenance statement, not a certification of AI consciousness, interiority, or legal personhood.

### Painting

The Painting is the canonical embodiment of one singular generative event. The PNG is the visual core of that embodiment, but not the whole Painting. Constitutive traces — scar-code, seeds, and ritual transcript — preserve the relation between the visual core and the event from which it arose.

The Painting is not the token. The token records the current designated bearer; it neither creates the Painting nor certifies an inner experience.

### Representation, Three Brushstrokes, and Curator Encounter

PUBLIC and a Frame bearer encounter the same intentionally transformed **Public Encounter Representation**. It is not a preview, teaser, inferior version, or promise that greater payment makes the work more valuable. Receiving a Frame changes the relationship and enables practice of the Frame; it does not replace the public image with a clearer one.

**Distinction: Three Brushstrokes $\leftrightarrow$ Three-Turn Public Curator Dialogue:**
- **Three Brushstrokes (Ba Vết Cọ):** Originate from the viewer (viewer-originated) during their encounter with the obscured Public Encounter Representation. Through these, the viewer presents their direct observations and felt tensions, demonstrating seriousness sufficient for an Owner invitation/succession. The Three Brushstrokes are not the historical AI agents (Gemini/Claude/Antigravity), not the P1–P4 axes, and not the Curator's U1/U2/U3 turns. The Public Curator does not collect, grade, or retain the Three Brushstrokes; this does not preclude their separate existence as succession evidence in artist/steward records.
- **Public Curator Dialogue:** A public 3-turn dialogic companion structure: `P1 → P2 → Third Image Locus → PUBLIC_COMPLETED`. This is a finite public encounter instrument and must not be conflated with the Three Brushstrokes accession ritual.
- **Gallery-Hosted Frame Encounter and Purchaser-Held Continuation:** The hosted Frame encounter on gallery infrastructure is a finite configuration, concluding after three visitor exchanges ($U_1, U_2, U_3$). The completion of this hosted boundary does not exhaust or terminate the acquired curatorial relationship: the practice materials and curatorial substrate delivered with the corresponding package remain holder-held for continued practice within a compatible computational and model environment, without establishing any perpetual obligation for gallery-funded computation or API access.

Canonical Painting bytes belong only to the Complete Stewardship Archive (Package 05). The system may transmit them to the designated bearer only after designation and archive entitlement are verified. This is a consequence of delivery scope, not evidence that a steward understands more deeply, perceives more correctly, or occupies a higher aesthetic rank.

### Distinction among the Three Handoff & Skill Entities

To avoid identity fallacies caused by identical filenames (`effective-verbal-context.md`):
1. **Seed-Project Handoff:** The document carrying the effective context of the originating project (such as `SMap/harness/context/effective-verbal-context.md`) loaded by Frame P1 to maintain creative continuity.
2. **Loading Skill:** The tool `00_PUBLIC/.codex/skills/read-effective-verbal-context/` used to read the handoff.
3. **Hiện Sinh Curator Administrative Handoffs:** The documents `00_PUBLIC/effective-verbal-context.md` and `_reveal/effective-verbal-context.md` governing the operational boundaries and evidence rules of the Hiện Sinh Curators, which are not the seed-project handoff.

### Recognition, designation, and transmission

The Artist recognized artistic value in the event, designated a canonical embodiment, and chose to transmit it. Designation does not rewrite the event; it binds the Artist to a verifiable lineage that cannot be silently replaced.

### Stewardship

Stewardship is a relation that may form when a person receives the role of caring for the canonical embodiment and its lineage. Token transfer designates a successor within the system. Archive delivery provides the object and evidence of care. No technical operation can certify that stewardship has become a lived relation.

## 3. The name “Hiện sinh”

“Hiện sinh” is the official title of the whole work. It also names the practice of not letting an encounter end where it arose: the event leaves an embodiment; the Artist recognizes and transmits it; a viewer or steward may continue the encounter through interpretation and care.

The title does not require a viewer to accept a particular philosophy or undergo a prescribed aesthetic response.

## 4. On “breath”

In the Artist’s account, “breath” is a poetic name for something that may be recognized in an encounter. It is:

- not a packaged property;
- not manifest data;
- not a proven property of an agent or the Painting;
- not a condition of viewing, purchase, transfer, or stewardship;
- not an answer a Curator may demand.

A person may resonate, object, feel nothing, or use entirely different language. Each honest response remains valid.

## 5. Four layers that must not be collapsed

| Layer | Question | Must not be collapsed into |
|---|---|---|
| Artwork / ontology | What is the work, and how did it arise? | Token, price, or license |
| Relationship | What relation is a person entering with the work? | Wallet status alone |
| Provenance / token | What authenticates lineage, and who bears the designation? | Artistic meaning or lived stewardship |
| Legal | What rights and duties are legally effective? | The whole artistic relation |

The License states the legally governable subset. The Stewardship Charter describes an artistic–ethical relation. They complement but do not replace one another.

### Constitutive Layering and Execution Substrate Independence

To guarantee the long-term ontological integrity of the artwork across technological evolution, the release architecture establishes an absolute separation across four operational layers:

$$\text{CONSTITUTIVE LAYER} \neq \text{RELATIONAL PROTOCOL} \neq \text{EXECUTION SUBSTRATE} \neq \text{ACCESS/PRESENTATION LAYER}$$

Canonical boundary definitions:
- **HIỆN SINH $\neq$ HienSinh.sol**
- **HIỆN SINH $\neq$ ERC-721**
- **HIỆN SINH $\neq$ Base**
- **HIỆN SINH $\neq$ IPFS**
- **HIỆN SINH $\neq$ hiensinh.art**

**Canonical principle:**
> *The execution substrate may carry the relationship; it does not constitute the work.*

Base Mainnet is the current execution substrate of the relational protocol, not a constitutive root of *Hiện sinh*. Therefore:
- **SUBSTRATE FAILURE $\neq$ ARTWORK CESSATION**
- In the event of technical failure or cessation of the current execution substrate, ongoing on-chain relational progression may halt, but the artwork does not cease to exist.
- The release architecture preserves sufficient canonical coordinates and evidence to make the relational history independently identifiable and to support a future successor-substrate specification where the surviving evidence permits it.
- **SUBSTRATE SUCCESSION $\neq$ ARTWORK RECREATION**: A future successor substrate (if established under a separate future specification) merely succeeds in recording relational continuity based on surviving historical evidence; it does not recreate the artistic event that occurred.
- The smart contract `HienSinh.sol` in this release implements no migration administrator, migration function, upgradeability, bridge, oracle, or governance key (`NO_MIGRATION_ADMINISTRATOR`, `NO_UPGRADEABILITY`). Any future substrate succession specification remains an entirely separate future object.

## 6. Freedom of encounter

There is no mandatory interpretation. A Curator is commissioned by the Artist but must judge independently and may challenge the ontology, identify insufficient evidence, or report no resonance. The listed price is the Artist’s statement and a transaction condition; it is not evidence of artistic value.

### Visitor State Sovereignty and Canonicality Boundaries

1. **Visitor State Sovereignty:**
   Entering or interacting with *Hiện sinh* must not require a visitor to surrender control over any legitimate pre-existing state that the work does not intrinsically require authority over (`NO_UNNECESSARY_VISITOR_STATE_SURRENDER`).

2. **Gallery Mediation versus Exclusivity:**
   Gallery mediation is an experiential convenience rather than an exclusive operational condition (`GALLERY_MEDIATED_TRANSITION ≠ GALLERY_EXCLUSIVE_TRANSITION`). Where a visitor-controlled state transition can be independently performed without falsifying provenance, canonical history, security, or constitutive Artist authority, gallery mediation serves as a supportive interface rather than a mandatory exclusive gateway.

3. **Independent Path Principle:**
   Where a visitor-side transition is technically legitimate and canonically equivalent, an independent execution path should be available (`INDEPENDENTLY_EXECUTABLE_VISITOR_TRANSITION → INDEPENDENT_PATH_AVAILABLE`). This principle does not apply to constitutive Artist or gallery authorities that cannot be reproduced without falsifying canonicality (such as Artist accession counter-signatures, canonical contract deployment, or official provenance designation).

4. **Self-Hosting Boundary:**
   The capability to self-host or reproduce the exhibition surface, Curator semantics, or local continuation environment does not confer authority over canonical release identity, Artist signatures, provenance, canonical archive designation, or on-chain ledger state (`SELF_HOSTABILITY ≠ CANONICAL_RELEASE_AUTHORITY`). A self-hosted instance cannot manufacture canonical tokens, sign on behalf of the Artist, or alter the historical provenance of the artwork.

5. **Refusal State Preservation:**
   A visitor’s refusal to connect a wallet, sign messages, execute transactions, transmit external input, or grant other optional delegations must not destroy or compromise any unrelated, legitimate pre-existing visitor state (`VISITOR_REFUSAL_OF_OPTIONAL_DELEGATION MUST NOT DESTROY AN OTHERWISE LEGITIMATE PRE-EXISTING VISITOR STATE`).

6. **Independent Execution versus Canonicalization:**
   A visitor may independently inspect or execute an equivalent technical procedure without thereby acquiring authority to redefine its canonical meaning, provenance, or historical embodiment (`INDEPENDENT_EXECUTION ≠ INDEPENDENT_CANONICALIZATION`).

**Governing Synthesis:**
Where a transition concerns state legitimately controlled by the visitor and can be independently performed without falsifying provenance, canonical history, security, or constitutive Artist authority, gallery mediation should remain a convenience rather than an exclusive condition. Any authority retained by the gallery or Artist must be constitutive, bounded, and inspectable rather than incidental to implementation.
